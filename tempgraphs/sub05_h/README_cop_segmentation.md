# Sub05_H CoP valley-to-peak segmentation

Generated by `refine_sub08_h_valleys.py`. Valleys and peaks are found in each limb's own CoP, not in vGRF.

- **Valley**: CoP minimum at foot contact. For the limb that steps first, the first valley is the low point its CoP climbs out of when leaving quiet standing.
- **Peak**: the highest CoP point of the stance, at push-off.
- **Step**: one valley to the next. **Stance** = valley to peak (saved). The swing after the peak is not saved.

Phase cuts are all on valleys, counted from the first one (v0):

| Phase | Span | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| QS | start to the rise out of quiet standing | |
| GI | rise to v0 | |
| SSSW | v0 to v5 | S1-S5 |
| SLT | v5 to v6 | T |
| SSLW | v6 to v11 | L1-L5 |
| GT | v11 to the end; the person comes to rest inside it | |

When the foot of L5 lands straight into standing the CoP has no valley there. That one cut then uses the foot-contact time (`GT_start_kind` in `phase_cuts.csv`).

Estimated samples (the `*_imputed` flags of the export, pale in the graphs) are never treated as measurements: `cuts_on_estimated_cop` in `phase_cuts.csv` names every cut that sits on estimated CoP, `stance_segments.csv` counts the estimated CoP / FMG / vGRF samples of each stance (`fully_measured` = 1 when there are none), and a limb whose walking CoP is mostly estimated is left UNRESOLVED.

The graphs follow the reference figure: sample axis, phase names on lines, arrowed dashed cuts and S1-S5 / T / L1-L5 labels. Valley and peak positions are not drawn; they are in `stance_segments.csv`.

Files:

- `<record>/trial_##_<l|r>_complete.png`: the graphs.
- `stance_segments/<record>/trial_##_<l|r>_<PHASE>_<S1..L5>.csv`: the samples of one stance, valley to peak.
- `stance_segments.csv`: valley, peak and next-valley time and sample of every saved stance.
- `phase_cuts.csv`, `phase_intervals.csv`: phase cuts per limb graph, in seconds and samples.

Phase labels in the segmented_data exports (`provisional_phase`) cover the whole phase span, swing included. Graphs without a valid ordered stance layout are marked UNRESOLVED rather than forced.

## Sub05_H run and source limitations

This run produced 80 full-trial limb graphs: 63 receive the CoP-derived layout and 17 are UNRESOLVED. For each resolved graph, eleven saved stances follow the 5 SSSW / 1 SLT / 5 SSLW count. See `phase_cuts.csv` for the status and reason of each limb.

Of the 63 count-valid layouts, 15 also require review under the checks below. Neither an unflagged graph nor a matching count is independent confirmation of a true gait event. Do not use these provisional labels as validated training ground truth.

A count-valid layout is not automatically an accurate gait annotation. Additional Sub05 QA flags weak stance climbs (<35% of that trial's median), unusually long intervals (>1.5 times its median), and paired SLT offsets outside 30-70% of that trial's median CoP stride. These are conservative review heuristics, not new segmentation rules or validated gait thresholds. Flagged graphs show REVIEW REQUIRED; `review_required` and `review_notes` in `phase_cuts.csv` explain each case. Candidate cuts are retained for inspection, not silently corrected or presented as ground truth.

The detector and thresholds are Ojaswi's existing approach, applied separately to each trial and limb; no fixed timing or Sub08 phase boundaries are copied. The 5-1-5 phase names are protocol assumptions, not independently measured short/long step lengths. CoP extrema are signal-derived event candidates, not independently verified heel strikes or push-off times.

Original sensor values, timestamps, missing-data flags and NaNs are preserved. Short gaps are interpolated in memory for detection and shown pale for display, not saved as recovered measurements. The graphs use display-only gains and offsets, not shared physical units.

The source synchronization report records a missing left-insole end trigger for `sub_21`, trial 16. Its trial endpoint was inferred during the original export using matched clock offsets; this rerun does not recover that trigger or realign the raw data.

The original `README.md`, provisional tables, manifests, verification reports and review sheets are retained as historical outputs; they describe the previous segmentation, not these CoP cuts. Unresolved source CSVs retain their previous phase columns for preservation; use the current `phase_cuts.csv` status as authoritative for this run, not those old labels.

Reproduce only this subject with `python -B refine_sub08_h_valleys.py Sub05_H` from the repository root. Legacy `generate_subject05.py` and resegmentation scripts can overwrite the new graphs and labels.
