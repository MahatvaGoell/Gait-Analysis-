# Sub05_H CoP valley-to-peak segmentation

Generated by `refine_sub08_h_valleys.py`. Valleys and peaks are found in each limb's own CoP, not in vGRF.

80 limb graphs: 68 cut, 12 UNRESOLVED, 24 of the cut graphs carry a review note.

- **Valley**: CoP minimum at foot contact. For the limb that steps first, the first valley is the low point its CoP climbs out of when leaving quiet standing. Where the CoP is held exactly constant while the foot is unloaded, the valley is the last sample of that flat floor.
- **Peak**: the highest CoP point of the stance, at push-off.
- **Step**: one valley to the next. **Stance** = valley to peak (saved). The swing after the peak is not saved.

Phase cuts are all on valleys, counted from the first one (v0):

| Phase | Span | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| QS | start to the rise out of quiet standing | |
| GI | rise to v0 | |
| SSSW | v0 to v5 | S1-S5 |
| SLT | v5 to v6 | T |
| SSLW | v6 to v11 | L1-L5 |
| GT | v11 to the end; the person comes to rest inside it, and any further closing step belongs to GT | |

This is the layout that reproduces the reviewer's marks on Sub08_H, sir_21, interval 04, left (cuts at samples 1005 / 1119 / 1722). No other recording has reviewer marks, so every other cut is a signal-derived candidate.

A first CoP climb counts as S1 only if the limb then lifts off. `S1_lift_off_ratio` is how far the limb's load drops after S1 relative to its next three swings: below 0.35 the climb is a weight shift made while still standing and stays in GI (`standing_shift_before_S1_left_in_GI`); between 0.35 and 0.60 S1 is kept and marked for review.

When the foot of L5 lands straight into standing the CoP has no valley there. That one cut then uses the foot-contact time (`GT_start_kind` in `phase_cuts.csv`).

## Cross-check between two detectors

The primary detector is in `refine_sub08_h_valleys.py`. A second, independently written detector (`refine_subject03_04.py`, exported by `export_second_engine_trains.py`) proposes its own candidates; they are re-placed on the valley and peak convention above. `cut_source` says which detector supplied a graph's cuts and `detector_cross_check` how the two compare:

- `detectors_agree`: 46 graphs
- `detectors_count_from_different_steps`: 6 graphs
- `neither_detector_found_11_stances`: 11 graphs
- `only_this_detector_found_11_stances`: 17 graphs

`not_run` means the second detector was not run for this subject. Where the two count from different first steps the graph is cut by the primary detector and marked for review; the disagreement is not resolved automatically.

## Review notes

`review_required` and `review_notes` in `phase_cuts.csv` (and a REVIEW line under the graph) mark layouts that deserve a look: detector disagreement, cuts on estimated CoP, hand-entered edges, a weak CoP climb (<35% of the trial median), an unusually long step (>1.5 times the trial median), GI longer than 1.5 median strides, or left/right SLT cuts that are not about half a stride apart. They are review heuristics and never move a cut. A matching 5-1-5 count or an unflagged graph is not a validation.

Estimated samples (the `*_imputed` flags of the export, pale in the graphs) are never treated as measurements: `cuts_on_estimated_cop` names every cut that sits on estimated CoP, `stance_segments.csv` counts the estimated CoP / FMG / vGRF samples of each stance (`fully_measured` = 1 when there are none), and a limb whose walking CoP is mostly estimated is left UNRESOLVED.

The graphs follow the reference figure: sample axis, phase names on lines, arrowed dashed cuts and S1-S5 / T / L1-L5 labels. They draw the exported values without smoothing; only a display offset and gain differ per signal. Valley and peak positions are not drawn; they are in `stance_segments.csv`.

## Files

- `<record>/trial_##_<l|r>_complete.png`: the graphs.
- `stance_segments/<record>/trial_##_<l|r>_<PHASE>_<S1..L5>.csv`: the samples of one stance, valley to peak, values as exported.
- `stance_segments.csv`: valley, peak and next-valley time and sample of every saved stance.
- `phase_cuts.csv`, `phase_intervals.csv`: phase cuts per limb graph, in seconds and samples.

Phase labels in the segmented_data exports (`provisional_phase`) cover the whole phase span, swing included, and match `phase_cuts.csv` for every cut graph. UNRESOLVED graphs get `provisional_phase` = UNRESOLVED. Graphs without a valid ordered stance layout are never forced.

Older files in this folder (previous README, provisional tables, manifests, verification reports, review sheets) describe earlier segmentations, not these cuts. Legacy generation or resegmentation scripts can overwrite these outputs.

Reproduce: `python export_second_engine_trains.py` (once), then `python refine_sub08_h_valleys.py Sub05_H`.
